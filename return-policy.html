Return & Exchange Policy

Home / Return Policy

The Short Version

We want you to be sure — not stuck.

If something isn't right, tell us within the window below and we'll sort out an exchange or refund. Here's exactly how it works.

01

7-Day Return Window

Items can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of purchase or delivery, with your original receipt or order confirmation.

02

Condition of the Item

The item must be unworn, unwashed and unaltered, with original tags and packaging still attached.

03

How to Start a Return

Call or message us on 0303 0581519 with your order details, and we'll guide you through drop-off or pickup.

04

Exchanges

Prefer a different size or colour? We'll happily exchange it, subject to stock availability at the time.

05

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method, or as store credit if you prefer.

06

Non-Returnable Items

Unstitched fabric that has been cut, and items marked "final sale," cannot be returned or exchanged.

Still unsure?
